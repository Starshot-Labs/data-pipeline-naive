Pipeline Viewer

Output of pipeline/run.mjs: the anchor and the LLM-placed object, both with their transform baked in. Older spec.json folders load too, unposed.

Run

Place with LLM asks the model alone, physics off. Apply Physics presses the pose currently on screen into mesh contact — no model call. Full Place does both. A local sample keeps its raw meshes and gains posed copies, so all of them are repeatable.

Show

A posed pair is shown exactly as the files say. A raw pair is parked side by side until something places it. Blocks overlays the merged decomposition the placement model reads; Voxels shows the raw occupancy it was merged from. After Full Place or Apply Physics, Ghost overlays a translucent copy of the placed object at the pose the physics started from.
